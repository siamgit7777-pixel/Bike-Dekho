Frequently Asked

What are the most important things I should know about riding a bike?
Safety First! Always obey the rules of the road. Obey all traffic signals, signs, and laws. Get in the mindset of “driving” your bike—not just “riding” your bike. This will help you be a more focused and legally compliant bike rider.
How can I tell if my helmet is old and I need a new one?
If your helmet has cracks, loose foam, damaged straps, or is over 3–5 years old, you should replace it. Any hard impact also means you need a new one immediately.
My bike has been in storage is it safe to ride?
If your bike was stored well and has no damage, it can be safe — just check the tires, brakes, chain, and frame first. If anything looks cracked, rusty, or not working smoothly, fix it before riding.
What rules should I follow when riding my bike?
Always wear a helmet, follow traffic rules, stay visible, and keep your bike in good working condition.
